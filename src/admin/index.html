League Information

Entry and Pots

The itemised parts of the entry fee as ordered label:amount pairs (for example Weekly payouts:3,Season payout:1,Ace pot:1). Shown beside the check-in payment options. A total that does not match the entry fee is flagged here, but never blocks the save.

Shown beside the player check-in payment options, so players can see plainly what the entry fee pays for. Leave blank to use the default: Your entry fee builds the weekly payout pool and pays the season payout. Second place takes its share back when enough players show; otherwise the winning team takes the whole pool.

Points

The per-league points table. Blank uses the default 1:2,2:1.5,3:1 with a 0.5 participation credit. A league that pays differently overrides it here.

The weekly payout pool. Every player contributes the per-player amount, so the pool is that amount times the number of players. Second place takes the flat amount back only when at least the minimum number of players show; the winning team takes the rest (or the whole pool below the threshold). Leave the contribution blank to pay no weekly money.
